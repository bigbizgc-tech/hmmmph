Your first call with GC: the prep file

Calling or texting (574) 213-9097 for the first time? Have these four answers ready and the call gets to the point fast. Each one has a shop answer and a streamer answer, so you can see the shape.

  1. What are you pushing, and where should people land?
    Shop: “Saturday brunch. The booking link in our bio.”
    Streamer: “Friday’s stream. My Twitch channel.”
  2. Who should see it?
    Shop: “People close enough to drive over.”
    Streamer: “People who watch the games I play.”
  3. Is there a date?
    An opening, an event, a stream night. If there is, get in touch at least a week ahead so the whole run lands before it.
  4. How big do you want to start?
    A test is Spark. A launch or a stream night is Boost. A date you only get once is Blitz. Every month is a plan. Free quote. Ask for price. on any of them.

The one-line brief. Put the four together and say it in one breath: “I want to push ___, to people who ___, landing on ___, by ___.” Start the call with that line.

What GC does with it. He tells you which file fits, or whether you need one at all. If your page isn’t ready for strangers yet, you hear that first.

Right after the call. After you send the form or call, we confirm the details and how you’d like to pay before anything starts. Nothing runs without your OK. For a boost, our target is live within 24 hours of payment.

The code

The rules we run by.

Six lines we hold to on every job, from a one-time Spark to a monthly plan.

  1. Your OK comes first

    Nothing runs under your name until you say go. You see what runs before it runs.

  2. You get a recap

    When a run ends you get the numbers that matter: what was spent, how many people it reached, and the clicks or views it got.

  3. Price before work

    Free quote. Ask for price. on any boost, plan or add-on, and you have it before anything starts. Quoted jobs follow the written quote you approve before work starts.

  4. No promised numbers

    Results depend on your content, your audience and the platform. No set number of views, likes or followers is guaranteed.

  5. Stay or walk

    Monthly plans run month to month. Cancel any time before your next billing date and you won’t be charged again. A month that has already started isn’t refunded.

  6. A local person answers

    Call or text (574) 213-9097. You deal with a person in South Bend.
