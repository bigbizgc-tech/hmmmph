The code

The rules we run by.

Six lines we hold to on every job, from a $19.99 Spark to a monthly plan.

  1. Your OK comes first

    Nothing runs under your name until you say go. You see what runs before it runs.

  2. You get a recap

    When a run ends you get the numbers that matter: what was spent, how many people it reached, and the clicks or views it got.

  3. Price before work

    Boosts, plans and add-ons have their price on the page. Quoted jobs follow the written quote you approve before work starts.

  4. No promised numbers

    Results depend on your content, your audience and the platform. No set number of views, likes or followers is guaranteed.

  5. Stay or walk

    Monthly plans run month to month. Cancel any time before your next billing date and you won’t be charged again. A month that has already started isn’t refunded.

  6. A local person answers

    Call or text (574) 213-9097. You deal with a person in South Bend.
