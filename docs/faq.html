Buying and paying

How to buy, where the money goes, and the refund policy word for word.

Back to the index
How do I buy a package?

Send the form on the contact page, or call or text (574) 213-9097. Tell us what you’re promoting and which package you want, and we’ll give you the price. The Ask for price button on any package page fills that package in for you.

After you send the form or call, we confirm the details and how you’d like to pay before anything starts. For boosts, our target is live within 24 hours of payment.

Link to this answer
Does my money go into a real ad?

Yes. Part of what you pay buys the ad itself, through the platform’s own ad tools. The rest covers setup, aiming it and your recap.

Every recap shows what the platform actually charged for the ad. What each number means.

Link to this answer
Why not just hit Promote myself?

You can. The Promote button in the app puts money behind a post, and sometimes that’s all a post needs. Here is what a package adds on top of the ad money:

  • Setup in the platform’s own ad tools, aimed at your area or the audience you name.
  • You see it and OK it before anything runs.
  • A recap when the run ends: what was spent, how many people it reached, and the clicks or views it got. Boost and Blitz add follows.
  • Spark comes with the 30-Day Post Kit, so new visitors land on a page that’s alive.
  • On a monthly plan, the aim is checked and adjusted every month as the numbers come in.

Rather run it yourself? How a push is aimed is written up on How it works. Use it on your own ads.

Link to this answer
What is your refund policy?

Word for word:

  • Boosts: Cancel before your ad is set up and you get a full refund. Ad money the platform has already spent can’t be returned. If we don’t deliver the run, you get back the setup part and any ad money the platform didn’t spend.
  • Monthly plans: Monthly plans run month to month. Cancel any time before your next billing date and you won’t be charged again. A month that has already started isn’t refunded.
  • Add-ons: If we haven’t started the work, you get a full refund. One round of edits is included once it’s delivered.
  • Quoted jobs: Quoted jobs follow the written quote you approve before work starts.
  • Rejected ads: If a platform rejects an ad, we fix it and resubmit. If it still can’t run, unspent ad money is refunded.

The full terms are on the terms and refunds page.

Link to this answer
How do you price websites and AI work?

Website Design, AI Consulting, AI Automation and Creator Content Systems are quoted per job. After a short call you get a written quote with the price and what’s included, before any work starts.

Quoted jobs follow the written quote you approve before work starts.

Section 02 · 6 answers

The ads

What runs, who sees it, where it runs and how fast.

Back to the index
Are these real ads? Who sees them?

Yes. Every push is a real paid placement, bought through the platform’s own ad tools, the same ones any business uses. No bots, no fake followers, no engagement pods.

It’s shown to the people you pick: your area, or the audience you name. Local shops usually aim by place. Creators usually aim by the kind of viewer.

Link to this answer
What can I promote? Do I need to make a new ad?

Your post, page, channel, website or booking line. A boost puts money behind something you already have, so you don’t need a new video to start.

If you want content made, the 30-Day Post Kit gives you a month of captions, and Total Blackout includes AI-made posts and promos.

Link to this answer
Which platforms do you run ads on?

X, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. X is the newest, and we’re booking it now.

Each boost runs on one platform you pick. Surge and Overdrive run on up to 2. If a platform is the wrong fit for you, we’ll tell you straight.

Link to this answer
Can you send people to my Twitch?

Yes. The ad runs on X, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or Facebook, and it can point people at your Twitch channel. Send us your channel link and your stream nights when you get in touch.

Boost is built for a launch, a drop or a stream night. Creator Content Systems sets up a system that turns every stream into a week of posts.

Link to this answer
How fast does a boost go live?

Our target is live within 24 hours of payment. For an event, book at least a week ahead so the whole run lands before the date. Monthly plans start with the intake call.

Link to this answer
What if a platform rejects my ad?

Every platform reviews ads before they run. If a platform rejects an ad, we fix it and resubmit. If it still can’t run, unspent ad money is refunded.

Results

What to expect, and what you get at the end.

Back to the index
How many views or followers will I get?

Results depend on your content, your audience and the platform. No set number of views, likes or followers is guaranteed.

Nobody honest can promise you a number. What you get is a real ad in front of real people, aimed where you want it, and a straight recap of what it did.

Link to this answer
What do I get at the end of a run?

A recap. Spark’s covers spend, reach, and clicks or views. Boost and Blitz add follows. Monthly plans get a recap every month with spend, reach, clicks or views, and follows.

Every recap shows what was spent and what it reached. What each number means.

Link to this answer
Is a small boost even worth it?

Spark is a test, and that’s its job. Part of what you pay buys the ad itself. The rest covers setup, aiming it and your recap. Real people see a real placement, and you find out whether a bigger push makes sense before you spend more.

Link to this answer
What if the ad doesn’t do much?

Then you learned something real for the price of a test, and we’ll tell you straight what we’d change: the post, the aim, the platform, or the page people land on.

Sometimes the honest answer is to fix your page before you spend another dollar. That’s what Profile Fix is for.

Monthly plans

Contracts, cancelling and how the three plans differ.

Back to the index
Is there a contract on the monthly plans?

No long contract. Monthly plans run month to month.

Just want one month? Surge, Overdrive and Total Blackout each come as one month only, billed once with no commitment.

Link to this answer
How do I cancel a plan?

Call, text or email us. Monthly plans run month to month. Cancel any time before your next billing date and you won’t be charged again. A month that has already started isn’t refunded.

The full terms are on the terms and refunds page.

Link to this answer
What’s the difference between the three plans?
  • Surge: paid promotion every month on up to 2 platforms, with a monthly recap.
  • Overdrive: everything in Surge, plus automatic posting and AI tools for the repeat work in your business.
  • Total Blackout: ads, AI-made posts, an AI assistant that answers your customers, and a clean web page, all set up and run for you.

You can move up any month. Free quote. Ask for price. on any of the three.

Not for Profile Fix. You publish the changes yourself, so nobody else needs your login.

What happens to what I send in the contact form?

It’s emailed to us so we can get back to you about what you asked. The privacy page covers what we collect and why.

What is the clearance meter on this site?

Every page here is a file. Open one and read it to the end and your clearance goes up. Higher levels open the classified blocks: the field notes on every package file, how a push is aimed, the first-call prep file, and at Level 5 the Vault.
