Classified Clearance 5 · Inner Circle

Rule 01: Fix the landing first

You already have this one. It was your Level 1 file: fix the landing before you buy the traffic. Every rule below assumes your page passes the three-second test.

Classified Clearance 5 · Inner Circle

Rule 02: Go where your people already scroll

You don’t need every platform. You need the one or two your people open without thinking about it.

A barbershop, a food truck and a streamer don’t need the same feed. Get good in one place before you spread yourself thin across five.

Do it today: ask your next ten customers which app they open first. Streaming? Ask chat which app they found you on. Put your effort there.

Classified Clearance 5 · Inner Circle

Rule 03: Put money behind what already works

A paid push puts a post in front of more people. It does the most for a post that already gave people a reason to stop: the clip that got shared, the post that brought messages, the moment chat lost it.

Look at what pulled on its own before you pay to push anything. If two posts are close, test both small and let the cheap test pick. That is the job of Spark: a small, real push that tells you whether a bigger one makes sense.

Do it today: scroll back through your last month of posts and clips. Find the one that did best with no money behind it. That is your first push.

Classified Clearance 5 · Inner Circle

Rule 04: Start the push before stream night

An ad for a stream night has to land before you go live. People need to see it, remember it and come back at the time you said.

Time the run so it ends on the night. Boost is a 5-day run, so start it about 5 days out. On TikTok it runs as a one-day burst, so run that the day before. Put the day, the time and your time zone in the ad, and point it at your channel, where the follow button is.

Do it today: pick your next big stream night, count back 5 days, and put “start the push” on that day.

Classified Clearance 5 · Inner Circle

Rule 05: Turn one stream into a week of clips

One stream holds a week of posts. The clutch play, the funny fail, the moment chat lost it. Each one is a clip that can bring someone to the next stream.

Mark the moments while you play, or right after. Cut each one down to the part worth watching, then post one a day until you go live again. A shop can do the same with one busy day filmed in short bits.

Do it today: after your next stream, write down five timestamps worth a clip. That is five days of posts. Creator Content Systems sets up the whole workflow if you’d rather not do it by hand.

Classified Clearance 5 · Inner Circle

Rule 06: Show up where your town shows up

Markets, game days, festivals, fundraisers, open mics, tournaments. When the town gathers, people are already in the mood to find something new.

Post before so people come, during so the people who missed it feel it, and after to thank everyone who showed up. If you’re putting money behind a date, start the push at least a week out so it lands while people are still making plans. Blitz is built for exactly that.

Do it today: put the next three local events you could show up at on your calendar, with one post planned for each.

Classified Clearance 5 · Inner Circle

Rule 07: Let happy customers do the talking

A post from a happy customer reaches the people who already trust that customer. That kind of word of mouth is easy to ask for and hard to fake.

Ask while they’re smiling: right after the haircut, the meal, the stream, the finished job. Make it easy with your handle on the counter, a QR code to your review page, and a repost every time someone tags you.

Never buy reviews and never write fake ones. It breaks the platforms’ rules, and people can tell.

Do it today: put your handle and one line, “Tag us and we’ll repost you,” where customers pay or wait.

Classified Clearance 5 · Inner Circle

Rule 08: Steady beats loud

Twenty posts in one week and then a month of silence looks like a business that closed. Three good posts a week, every week, looks like a business that’s open.

Pick a pace you can hold on your busiest, worst week. Film in batches on one day, then post through the rest of the week.

Do it today: choose your posting days and put them on the calendar for the next four weeks. The 30-Day Post Kit does the planning for you.

Classified Clearance 5 · Inner Circle

Rule 09: Answer while they’re still looking

A push brings messages, comments and questions. The person asking “are you open Sunday?” is ready right now. By tomorrow they may have moved on.

Answer the same day, in plain words, and put the next step in the reply: the booking link, the address, the stream time.

Do it today: write answers to the five questions you get most and keep them in your phone’s notes, ready to paste. AI Automation can send those answers for you.

Classified Clearance 5 · Inner Circle

Rule 10: Own a list the app can’t take

Followers live on someone else’s app. If the app changes its rules or your account gets locked, the way you reach them goes too.

Give people one way to stay in touch that belongs to you: an email list, a text list, or a Discord for your community. Ask where they already trust you: at the counter, at the end of a stream, in the follow-up after a sale.

Do it today: pick one list and make signing up a single tap. Mention it once at the counter, or once a stream.

That’s the playbook. Ten rules, no secrets. The hard part is doing them every week while you run the business.
