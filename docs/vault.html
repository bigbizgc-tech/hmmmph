Clearance 5 · The Syndicate Playbook

Ten rules for getting seen.

The rules we run every push on, written for local shops, solo businesses and creators. Use them yourself. They work with or without us.

Classified Clearance 5 · Inner Circle

Rule 01: Fix the landing before you buy the traffic

Before you put a dollar behind a post, open your own profile like a stranger would.

In three seconds they should know three things: what you do, where you are, and what to do next. Follow, book, buy, show up.

If any of those is missing, an ad just sends more people to a page that loses them. Fix the bio. Pin your best post. Put one link in your bio, not five. That is the whole reason Profile Fix exists.

Do it today: hand your phone to someone who doesn’t know your business. Give them three seconds on your profile, then ask what you do.

Classified Clearance 5 · Inner Circle

Rule 02: One ad, one job

Decide what the ad is for before you make it. New followers. People in the door this weekend. Booked appointments. New faces in chat on stream night.

Each of those gets set up differently on the platform and gets judged by a different number. An ad that tries to do all of them at once does none of them well.

Do it today: finish this sentence before you spend anything: “This ad worked if ___ happened.” If you can’t fill in the blank, you aren’t ready to spend.

Classified Clearance 5 · Inner Circle

Rule 03: Win the first two seconds

People decide fast whether to keep watching. Open on the best part: the finished cut, the full room, the plate hitting the table, the clutch play, the punchline.

Skip the logo intro and the slow hello. Put the point in words on screen too, for anyone scrolling with the sound off.

Do it today: pause your next video on its first frame. If that still picture wouldn’t make you stop scrolling, start the video somewhere else.

Classified Clearance 5 · Inner Circle

Rule 04: Ask for one thing

Every post and every ad gets one call to action. Follow. Book. Call. Show up Saturday. Pick the one that matches the job from Rule 02.

Two asks split attention. Three asks get ignored. The words on screen, the caption and the button should all point at the same thing.

Do it today: read your last three captions. Count the asks. Cut each one down to a single ask.

Classified Clearance 5 · Inner Circle

Rule 05: Go where your buyers already scroll

You don’t need every platform. You need the one or two your customers open without thinking about it.

A barbershop, a food truck and a streamer don’t need the same feed. Get good in one place before you spread yourself thin across five.

Do it today: ask your next ten customers which app they open first. Put your effort there.

Classified Clearance 5 · Inner Circle

Rule 06: Let happy customers do the talking

A post from a happy customer reaches the people who already trust that customer. That kind of word of mouth is easy to ask for and hard to fake.

Ask while they’re smiling: right after the haircut, the meal, the stream, the finished job. Make it easy with your handle on the counter, a QR code to your review page, and a repost every time someone tags you.

Never buy reviews and never write fake ones. It breaks the platforms’ rules, and people can tell.

Do it today: put your handle and one line, “Tag us and we’ll repost you,” where customers pay or wait.

Classified Clearance 5 · Inner Circle

Rule 07: Show up where your town shows up

Markets, game days, festivals, fundraisers, open mics, tournaments. When the town gathers, people are already in the mood to find something new.

Post before so people come, during so the people who missed it feel it, and after to thank everyone who showed up. If you’re putting money behind a date, start the push at least a week out so it lands while people are still making plans. Blitz is built for exactly that.

Do it today: put the next three local events you could show up at on your calendar, with one post planned for each.

Classified Clearance 5 · Inner Circle

Rule 08: Steady beats loud

Twenty posts in one week and then a month of silence looks like a business that closed. Three good posts a week, every week, looks like a business that’s open.

Pick a pace you can hold on your busiest, worst week. Film in batches on one day, then post through the rest of the week.

Do it today: choose your posting days and put them on the calendar for the next four weeks. The 30-Day Post Kit does the planning for you.

Classified Clearance 5 · Inner Circle

Rule 09: Test small before you go big

Before real money goes behind anything, run a small push and watch what happens. If you have two versions of a post, test both small.

Let the cheap test pick the winner, then put the bigger money behind the one that already worked. That is the whole job of Spark: a small, real push that tells you whether a bigger one makes sense.

Do it today: look back at your last month of posts and find the one that did best on its own. That is your first test.

Classified Clearance 5 · Inner Circle

Rule 10: Read the recap and change one thing

Every run ends with numbers: what was spent, how many people it reached, and what they did. Read them. That is where the next push gets better.

Then change one thing for the next run: the hook, the picture, the audience, the day or the ask. Change everything at once and you’ll never know what worked.

Do it today: start a note on your phone called “What we changed.” One line per run: the date, what you changed, what happened.

That’s the playbook. Ten rules, no secrets. The hard part is doing them every week while you run the business.
