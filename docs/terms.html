Who you’re dealing with

These terms cover anything you buy from Blackout Syndicate, a GC General Contracting LLC company in South Bend, Indiana, run by founder Gregory Clark. “We” and “us” means Blackout Syndicate. “You” means the person or business buying.

Paying for a package or service means you agree to these terms. For quoted work, the written quote you approve is part of the deal too.

You need to be at least 18 to buy, or have a parent or guardian handle it for you.

06↑ Index

Platform rules and rejected ads

Every ad runs through the platform’s own ad tools, so it has to follow that platform’s ad rules. Each platform reviews ads before they run, and it can reject an ad or stop one. The platforms also change their rules, review times and prices on their own. We don’t control that.

If a platform rejects an ad, we fix it and resubmit. If it still can’t run, unspent ad money is refunded.

We won’t run an ad that breaks a platform’s rules or the law, even if you ask.

07↑ Index

No guaranteed results

Results depend on your content, your audience and the platform. No set number of views, likes or followers is guaranteed.

Nobody honest can promise you a number. What you get is a real paid placement, bought through the platform’s own ad tools, aimed where we agreed, and a recap of what was spent and what it reached.

08↑ Index

Your OK before anything runs

Nothing runs under your name until you say go. That covers ads, posts, AI replies and automations. You see it first and you approve it first.

Want something changed? Tell us before you approve it, and we’ll change it.

09↑ Index

Your part

  • Give us accurate information. What you sell, your prices, your hours or area, and the links you want promoted.
  • Only send what you have the rights to. Photos, video, music, logos and words you send us must be yours, or cleared for you to use in ads.
  • Keep it allowed. What you sell and what the ad says has to be legal and allowed on the platform.
  • Answer when we ask for your OK. Nothing runs until you approve it, so a slow answer means a slower start.

Before anything is set up, we go over exactly what access your package needs. Profile Fix needs no login at all. Your accounts stay yours.

If a claim comes up because of something you sent us without the rights to use it, that is your responsibility.

Who owns what

  • Your content stays yours. Your photos, videos, posts, logo, name and accounts belong to you. We use them only for the work you approve.

Limits on what we’re responsible for

We do the work we agree to, with care, and we’ll tell you straight if something goes wrong. Some things are out of our hands, and we aren’t responsible for them:

  • What the platforms do: rejecting or stopping ads, changing rules or prices, outages, or limiting an account
  • Results: views, likes, followers, sales or bookings
  • Problems caused by information or content you gave us

If we get something wrong, the most we owe you is what you paid us for that piece of work. For a monthly plan, that means the month it happened in. We aren’t responsible for indirect losses, like lost sales or lost profit.

Nothing here takes away a right the law says can’t be taken away.
