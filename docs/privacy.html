Who we are

Blackout Syndicate is a small marketing business in South Bend, Indiana. We run paid social media ads, content and AI setup for creators, streamers, solo businesses and local shops. Blackout Syndicate is a GC General Contracting LLC company, run by founder Gregory Clark.

This policy covers this website, www.blackoutsyn.com, and what happens to anything you send us through it. When we say “we” or “us”, we mean Blackout Syndicate.

Questions about your information go to info@blackoutsyn.com or (574) 213-9097, by call or text.

What the contact form collects

The form on the contact page asks for:

  • Your name (required)
  • Your email (required)
  • Your business or channel name
  • Your phone number, if you want a call or a text
  • The package or service you’re asking about (required, and “Not sure yet” counts)
  • The platform you want to run on
  • How you’d like us to reach you: call, text or email
  • Your message

The form also notes which page it was sent from and the last package page you looked at. If you came to the site from one of our ads, it also sends the tags that were on that ad’s link, like X’s click ID and the campaign name, so we know which ad brought you. Everything not marked required is up to you. Leave out anything you’d rather not share.

How it reaches us

When you press send, the form goes over an encrypted connection to a third-party form-delivery service, which emails it to our inbox at info@blackoutsyn.com. Right now that service is FormSubmit. It handles your message under its own terms and privacy policy. If we switch to a different delivery service, we’ll update this page.

What we use it for

  • To answer your question or send you a quote
  • To confirm the details and how you’d like to pay, before anything starts
  • To set up and run the work you approve
  • To send your recaps

Sending the form doesn’t put you on a mailing list.

If you call, text or email us, we get your phone number or email address and whatever you tell us. We use it the same way: to answer you and do the work.

The booking calendar

The booking calendar on some pages is Google Calendar’s booking page, shown inside this site. If you book a time, what you type into it goes to Google and to us, so we can hold the slot and reach you. Google handles it under its own privacy policy.

Booking is optional. You can always use the form, call or text instead.

The X Pixel

This site uses the X Pixel, a small piece of code from X (the platform that used to be Twitter). We run ads on X, and the pixel tells us whether those ads lead to real visits and real inquiries.

What it measures

  • Visits to pages on this site
  • Views of package and service pages, with the name of the package
  • Visits to the contact page
  • A successful form send and the thank-you page after it, with the package you picked
  • Taps on our call, text and email links, with the page or package they were on

What X can see

  • Cookies or similar identifiers the pixel sets in your browser
  • Your IP address, browser and device type
  • The page you’re on and the page that sent you
  • Your X account, if you’re logged in to X

What we never pass to X

  • Your name
  • Your email or phone number in readable form
  • Your message
  • Your clearance file

When you send the form

When you send the contact form, our own server also tells X that a form was sent. It includes your X ad-click ID if you came from an X ad, your IP address and browser, and a scrambled (hashed) version of your email and phone number. X uses that only to match the form send to the ad. X can’t read a hashed email or phone number, and we send none of the message you wrote.

X handles pixel data under its own privacy policy. What we get back is reporting in our X ads account, like how many visits or form sends followed an ad. Those reports don’t tell us who you are.

How to limit it

  • On X: open Settings and privacy, then Privacy and safety. Under Ads preferences, turn off personalized ads. The data sharing settings on the same screen limit it further. Your X privacy settings.
  • In your browser: block third-party cookies, clear cookies for this site, or use a content blocker that stops tracking pixels.

Blocking the pixel doesn’t break anything here. Every page still works.

The clearance game

As you read this site, you earn clearance and open classified files. Your progress is saved in your own browser, in a spot called local storage. It stays on your device and never reaches us. It holds:

  • Your clearance points and level
  • Which files you opened and which you read to the end
  • Which hidden marks you found
  • Whether you found your package match, sent the form or said the name on the door
  • Which classified seals you broke early

Local storage also keeps three small notes, only if you use the part of the site they belong to: your answers to the package match, the boxes you ticked on the what we need from you checklist, and the name and business you type on your Syndicate ID card.

A few notes are kept for the current browser tab only, in session storage. They clear when you close the tab:

  • The last package page you looked at, so the form can fill it in for you
  • If you came from one of our ads, the tags on that ad’s link, so the form can tell us which ad it was
  • How you’d like us to reach you, and after you send the form, your first name and the package you picked, so the thank-you page can greet you
  • A note so a thank-you visit is only counted once

To wipe it: open the clearance panel with the LV meter at the top of the page and press Burn my file at the bottom. That clears your clearance and your match answers. Clearing this site’s data in your browser clears everything listed here.

Fonts and hosting

The lettering on this site comes from Google Fonts. To show it, your browser asks Google’s servers for the font files, which tells Google your IP address and browser details. Google handles that under its privacy policy. If the fonts are blocked, the site falls back to fonts already on your device.

Like any website, the service that hosts this site may keep standard server logs: IP address, browser type, the page asked for and the time. Those logs are used to run and protect the site.

Besides the X Pixel, this site runs no other tracking or analytics.

Links to YouTube and social platforms

This site links out to our pages on X, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and Discord, and to videos on YouTube. A link takes you to that platform, and what happens there is governed by that platform’s own privacy policy and settings.

If you hire us

To run the work you approve, we share what that work needs with the platforms and tools it runs on. For an ad, that means the post, page or link being promoted, the ad itself and who it should reach. Each platform handles that under its own policies.

Before anything is set up, we go over exactly what access your package needs. Your accounts stay yours. Work like automatic posting or an AI assistant is set up the way we agree with you first.

This website doesn’t collect card numbers. When it’s time to pay, we confirm how you’d like to pay before anything starts.

We don’t sell your information

We don’t sell your personal information. We don’t rent it or trade it either.

The only outside companies that handle it are the ones named on this page: the form-delivery service, our email and phone providers, X for the pixel, Google for the fonts and the booking calendar, our web host, and the platforms that run the work you approve. We would also share information if the law required it.

11↑ Index

How long we keep it

  • Inquiries and messages: as long as we need them to answer you and do the work. After that, ask and we’ll delete them.
  • Records the law makes a business keep, like payment records for taxes: as long as the law requires.
  • Your clearance file: in your browser until you burn it or clear this site’s data.
  • Tab notes: until you close the tab.

Your choices

  • Ask what we have. We’ll tell you what we hold about you.
  • Fix it. Tell us what’s wrong and we’ll correct it.
  • Delete it. Ask and we’ll delete your inquiry and contact details, except records the law makes us keep.
  • Limit the X Pixel. Use your X settings or your browser settings, as explained in The X Pixel.
  • Burn your clearance. Burn my file, at the bottom of the clearance panel.

To ask, email info@blackoutsyn.com or call or text (574) 213-9097. We may ask you to confirm it’s you before we hand over or delete anything.
