What it is

The short version.

Spark puts a real paid placement behind one post, page or channel on the platform you pick. It is the cheapest way to find out what happens when people who don’t follow you yet actually see your work.

Results depend on your content, your audience and the platform. No set number of views, likes or followers is guaranteed.

What you get

  • Setup of one paid promotion on one platform you pick
  • About $10 goes straight into the ad itself
  • A 3-day run aimed at your area or the audience you name
  • The 30-Day Post Kit, so you have something to post while new people find you
  • A short recap: spend, reach, and clicks or views

How it runs

From hello to live.

  1. Claim it

    Send the form or call. Tell us your name, what you’re promoting and the platform you want.

  2. Point it

    Your post, page, channel, site or booking line. Pick where you want new people to land.

  3. It goes live

    We set up a real paid placement through the platform’s own ad tools. Our target is live within 24 hours of payment.

  4. Read the recap

    When the run ends you get the numbers that matter: what was spent, how many people it reached, and the clicks or views it got.

Straight talk

Is this your file?

It’s for you if

  • You have never run a paid ad and want to see how it feels
  • One post deserves more eyes than it got
  • You want new viewers, not bot followers

It’s not for you if

  • You need customers coming in every month. Look at Surge
  • You want guaranteed numbers. Nobody honest can promise those

The math on Spark

  • $19.99 total. About $10 buys the ad, the rest covers setup, aiming it and your recap.
  • Over a 3-day run that is about $3.33 a day of real ad money.
  • The 30-Day Post Kit is included. On its own it costs $19.99, so the push is the bonus.
  • Platforms spend the first part of any run learning who responds. That is one reason longer runs tend to get more out of each dollar.

Questions

Asked and answered.

Every other question
Is $10 of ad money worth it?

At this size it is a test. Real people see a real placement, and you find out whether a bigger push makes sense. That is the whole job of Spark.

How does the ad actually run?

Through the platform’s own ad tools, the same ones any business uses. Nothing runs without your OK.

Will I get followers?

Maybe. Spark puts you in front of real people. Whether they follow depends on your content, your audience and the platform.
