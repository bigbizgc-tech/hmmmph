Playbook Rule 01: Fix the landing before you buy the traffic

Before you put one dollar behind a post, open your own profile like a stranger would.

In three seconds they should know three things: what you do, where you are, and what to do next. Follow, book, buy, show up.

If any of those is missing, an ad just sends more people to a page that loses them. Fix the bio first. Pin your best post. Put one link in your bio, not five.

That is the whole reason Profile Fix exists. Do it yourself or let us do it. Just don’t skip it.
