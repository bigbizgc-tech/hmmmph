Blackout Syndicate · Logo system · Final

The man
in the hat

The man in the hat was Blackout Syndicate's first logo: head down, face lost under the brim, nothing showing but the grin.
Same man, now a real logo: flat, three colours, sharp at 16 px, at home on black and on paper.

→

What stayed, what changed

  • Kept: the black fedora with its pinched two-lobe crown and broad, cocked brim, the gold band, the face lost in shadow, the white grin and its sparkle, the suit V.
  • Now: he stands in a gold disc of light, so a black hat shows on a black page with no outlines. The redacted face matches the redacted name. The light says Get Seen.
  • Flat: three colours, no gradients, glows or grey shading. The grin has 5 bold teeth on top, 4 below, and the smirk rises to the right.
  • Type: Cinzel is retired. The name uses the site's own Blacked Out lockup in Big Shoulders. BLACKOUT SYNDICATE moves off the hatband, except on signage and merch.

Colourways

-gold on dark · -white on photos · -black on paper

The man is always the darkest thing in the logo and the grin is always the brightest. -gold is three colours for dark grounds. -white follows the light: the disc and the grin are white and the man is cut out. -black follows the shadow: the man is ink inside an ink ring and the grin, band and collar stay paper. On paper the bar inverts, so it reads as an ink bar with paper letters.

Hero form

Rim light · lettered band · hero surfaces only

hero-rimlight.svg · the site hero, end cards, posters. The glow is a separate, optional layer.

Gold is spent sparingly

  • The coin (gold disc) is for the avatar, favicon, nav, stickers and merch: small, everywhere.
  • Hero surfaces (site hero, end cards, posters over the Chicago skyline) use the void man in rim light, lit by the searchlight, instead of a big gold plate.
  • The lettered hatband (BLACKOUT SYNDICATE on the band, as in Pack B) is for heroes, signage and merch at 200 px / 2 in and up. It never appears at working sizes.
  • Never use the rim-light man as the avatar or the nav mark. At 40 px it turns into a dark smudge.

Clear space & minimum sizes

x = half the height of the BLACKOUT bar

Stacked: keep x clear on every side. x is half the bar's height, about 1/8 of its width. For the coin alone, x is 1/6 of the disc.

Horizontal: x is a quarter of the coin's height.

Do & don't

Gold coin on dark

-black on paper

Coin on white is fine

Rim light on heroes

Gold type on paper

The paper bar disappears and gold is too faint. Use -black.

Show his face

No eyes, no features. Only the grin.

Restyle the parts

Don't recolour, rotate, outline, glow or stretch the coin, and never drop the band.

Badge as avatar

The seal is for stickers and stamps. At 40 px its ring text turns to noise. Avatars use the coin.
