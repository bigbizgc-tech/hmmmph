The final logo

Blacked Out.

BLACKOUT is cut out of a redaction bar, so its letters are made of the dark. SYNDICATE stands under it in gold light. Read top to bottom, the name goes from hidden to seen, which is the whole business: you’re in the dark until the light finds you. The B cell, half redacted and half lit, carries the same story down to a 16 px favicon.

01 · Primary

The stacked lockup

The primary logo for anything big: end cards, the site hero, flyers, shirts. BLACKOUT is Big Shoulders Display Black cut out of a paper bar. SYNDICATE is Big Shoulders ExtraBold, set to the exact width of the bar with optical spacing around the I. In the gold colourway the letters carry their own #070708 ink, so the logo holds over photos and skylines.

04 · The mark

One cell of the bar

The first cell of the redaction bar: a B cut out, redacted on top, in the light below. It is one letter, never a “BS” monogram. Use mark at 48 px and up. Below that, use mark-small, redrawn on a 32-unit grid with stems on whole pixels so it stays sharp at 16 px.

05 · The stamp

Clearance stamp

A plain ring with the bar slapped across it: SYNDICATE over the top, SOUTH BEND · INDIANA underneath, the snap-brim above the bar and “Get Seen.” below it. It’s for stickers, merch, packaging and the site’s “clearance granted” moment. It has no stars, no flutes and no red. The Bodoni is thickened slightly so the hairlines survive print. Use it at 96 px or 1 inch and larger, and never as the avatar.

06 · Secondary icon

The snap-brim

The site’s old gold fedora, redrawn as a 1930s Chicago snap-brim: the brim is 1.5 times the crown, the sides are flat, the front is snapped down and the whole hat is cocked 4°. It is one flat shape with the band cut out. It is the hidden mark in the site’s clearance game, and it’s a merch and sticker icon for the streamer side. It never sits next to the name as a lockup, and it never replaces the B.

10 · Do & don’t

Keep it clean

✓ Do · Gold on black

The default. On dark photos and on the skyline, use the gold files, which carry their own ink.

✓ Do · Black on paper

Use it for invoices, proposals, the Google Business Profile and chamber material. Plain and trustworthy.

✓ Do · White over images

Use one colour white on busy video and photos, and on dark shirts.

✕ Don’t · Gold files on white

The paper parts vanish. On light backgrounds, use -black.

✕ Don’t · Recolour

No red bars and no new colours. Red is the site’s stamp colour.

✕ Don’t · Stretch or squash

Scale it proportionally, always.

BS
✕ Don’t · A “BS” monogram

The mark is one letter, B.

✕ Don’t · Effects on the logo

No glows, shadows or outlines on the logo itself. Light the scene behind it instead.

✕ Don’t · The hat as a lockup

The snap-brim stands alone. It never sits beside the name or the B.
